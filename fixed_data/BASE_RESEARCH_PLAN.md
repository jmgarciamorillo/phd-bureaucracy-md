# Base Research Plan

> **Template / Example File:** This document contains the baseline research plan, objectives, methodology, and multi-year milestone schedule established at the beginning of the PhD. It can be written in an informal style. It serves as the seed to create initial research and training plans.

---

## 1. Research Proposal Summary

* **General Objective:** Develop and implement a hybrid computational framework combining hydrodynamic and magnetohydrodynamic simulations with deep neural network models to characterize particle acceleration and non-thermal radiation in the outer regions of pulsar magnetospheres.

---

## 2. Specific Objectives

1. **Objective 1:** Comprehensive literature review and setup of numerical magnetospheric simulation environments.
2. **Objective 2:** Implementation and optimization of a numerical solver for hydrodynamics and particle transport in pulsar environments.
3. **Objective 3:** Design and training of surrogate models to accelerate the computation of emission spectra.
4. **Objective 4:** Validation of the computational model against public observations from gamma-ray observatories.
5. **Objective 5:** Drafting of the Doctoral Thesis, as well as preparation of scientific publications in high-impact journals.

---

## 3. Methodology & Tools

* **Numerical Methods:** Codes, solvers, and models for simulating hydrodynamics and particle transport.
* **Machine Learning & Computation:** PyTorch / TensorFlow, GPU acceleration (CUDA), and high-performance computing on institutional clusters.
* **Observational Datasets:** Public and private datasets from gamma-ray observatories such as MAGIC, CTAO, VERITAS, among others.
